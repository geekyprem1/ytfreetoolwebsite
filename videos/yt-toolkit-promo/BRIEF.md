## Intent

30-second launch promo for YT Toolkit (yttools.pro) — the free, no-login YouTube creator
toolkit (60 tools including 15 calculators: extractors, AI generators, SEO helpers, stats,
calculators).
Concept from the pitch round: one URL gets pasted and the site's real results cascade
back (tags, transcript, AI titles, thumbnail, stats) — the whole toolkit proven in one
continuous flow — closing on the punch: 60 tools, $0, no login. Sell, not show: the
site's own captured screens are the video's hero assets. Tone: fast, confident, a little
cheeky — the energy of results arriving instantly.

Typical direction deliberately left behind: the feature-grid parade with a "meet the
ultimate free toolkit" voiceover.

## Assets

- None supplied by the user — the live-site capture (https://www.yttools.pro) supplies
  brand tokens, screens, and the product's real UI as the featured assets.

## Customizations

- Real captured screens staged as the featured visuals (not rebuilt mockups)
- Cuts on the music's beat; captions on (sound-off-safe for embeds)

## Notes

- User's brief: "This is my product, create a 30 sec video... give me your best!" —
  treated as an autonomous signal (full creative control; no checkpoint questions; the
  one kept question is preview-first-or-render at the end).
- Design: style_preset `broadside` (user's remembered pick, confirmed in uploadiq-promo
  runs), remixed onto yttools.pro's captured colors/fonts.
- Engines: offline — Kokoro voice, MusicGen music (HeyGen not signed in at run time).
- 30s hard target; avoid feature-parade pacing — outcomes over enumeration, VO ≈70 words.
